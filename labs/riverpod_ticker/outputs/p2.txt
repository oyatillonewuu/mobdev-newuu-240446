1. The feed is behind its provider and encapsulated with yet
   another provider because of fundamentally different
   functions the two server:
    1. First provider provides us with TickFeed object and
       updates on its state.
    2. Second provider gives us StreamProvider which grabs data
       from internal function of the TickFeed object.

    So, first one gives us the object and its state, and the
    second one gives the stream of values produced by that
    object (TickFeed).
2. Disposes the AsyncValue stream on List<Tick> of TickFeed provider.
3. AsyncLoading because the value hasn't been loaded yet and `hasValue`
